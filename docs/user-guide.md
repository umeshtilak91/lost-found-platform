# User guide

Lost & Found helps people who lost something find the person who found it,
and the reverse, without exposing anyone's phone number or email until both
sides are ready.

## Signing in

1. Choose **Sign in** and enter your email address.
2. We email you a 6-digit code. Enter it on the same screen. Codes expire after
   a few minutes and work once. You can ask for a new code after a short wait.
3. If Google sign-in is offered on the page, you can use that instead.

You can browse and search without signing in. Signing in is needed to post,
respond, pay or message.

## Reporting a lost item

1. Choose **Report an item** → **I lost something**.
2. Describe it (what it is, category, a short description, the date you lost it,
   and photos if you have them).
3. **Where was the item lost or found?** Start typing a place and pick a
   suggestion, or use **Use my location**. Confirm the location before
   publishing. Your exact position is never shown; others see the area.
4. Add an **ownership question**: something only the real owner would know (a
   sticker, scratch, wallpaper, contents). Finders answer it privately.
5. Acknowledge the **finder reward** for the category (see below) and publish.

## Reporting a found item

Choose **Report an item** → **I found something** and fill in the same details. Keep
identifying details (serial numbers, ID contents) out of the public
description and use them in the ownership question instead. Finders never pay
anything.

## Searching by location

On the home page, **Lost items**, **Found items** or **Browse**, type what you're looking
for and a place (or **Use my location**), then pick a distance: 2, 5 (default),
10 or 25 km, or **Anywhere**. Results can be sorted by distance. Distances are
approximate on purpose. If location suggestions are unavailable, typed place
names still work. If you deny the browser's location permission, the page
explains how to type a place instead.

## Claims and ownership checks

- **On a found listing**, the owner chooses **This could be mine** and answers
  the finder's question. Only the finder sees the answer.
- **On a lost listing**, the finder chooses **I found this item** and answers
  the owner's question. Only the owner sees the answer.
- The person who posted the listing reviews answers and **accepts** the one
  that fits, or declines it. You can't claim your own listing.
- Don't put phone numbers, emails, UPI IDs or links in answers or
  descriptions. They are blocked until the connection is paid.

## The ₹100 connection fee

After a match is accepted, the **owner** pays a one-time **₹100 connection fee**
to the platform. It unlocks private chat (and optional phone sharing) for that
match only. Payment runs through Razorpay. The fee is the only money the
platform collects. Paying it does **not** mark the item returned.

If the match ends before you could use it (for example, you found the item
yourself), you can ask for a refund through support.

## The finder reward is paid directly

Each category has a fixed **finder reward** (for example, ₹500 for a phone and
₹300 for a wallet; see **Pricing**). The owner agrees to it when posting or
claiming, and the amount is locked for that match. The owner pays it **directly
to the finder** (UPI, cash or any way you both agree) at handover. The platform
does not hold, collect or forward reward money. After the return, the finder
can record "Finder confirmed receipt" or report a payment issue.

## When contact information becomes available

| Stage | What each side sees |
|---|---|
| Before a match is accepted | The listing, the poster's first name and last initial, the approximate area |
| Accepted, fee not paid | Match status; chat stays locked |
| Fee paid | Private chat for this match; phone number only if that person turns on phone sharing |

Email addresses are never shown to other users.

## Handover and receipt

1. Arrange the handover in chat. Status: **Return being arranged**.
2. After handing the item over, the finder chooses **I handed over this item**.
   Status: **Awaiting owner confirmation**. The owner gets reminders after 48
   hours and 7 days, but it is never confirmed automatically.
3. The owner chooses **Have you received your item?** → **Yes, I received it**.
   Status: **Returned**. Choose **There is a problem** instead if something is wrong.
4. Other options: **I found it myself** (owner) and **Returned outside the
   platform** (finder) close the listing without a match.

## Where completed items appear

Returned and closed listings leave active search. Both people see the return
under **My account → Completed returns**, with the conversation (still readable),
the reward status and the fee receipt. If a return was marked by mistake, use
**Request a correction** there.

## Getting help and reporting abuse

- Use **Report listing** on any listing to flag spam, scams, personal information or
  duplicates. Moderators review every report.
- For payment, refund or return problems, use **There is a problem**, the
  refund request on a match, or **Request a correction**. These reach the
  support queue.
- To export or delete your data, go to **My account**.
